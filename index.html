Questions

Good to know.

Where exactly is the studio?

Downtown Dallas. The exact address and access details arrive automatically the moment your booking is confirmed.

What does it cost?

$60/hr on weekdays, $75/hr on weekends. Half days (4 hours) are $240 weekday / $300 weekend; full days (8 hours) are $480 weekday / $600 weekend. Paid by card at booking — no deposits, no back-and-forth. Here's how that compares with other Dallas studios.

What's included — and what should I bring?

The room: natural light, hardwood floors, track lighting, and kitchen access. Pro lighting rents as add-ons at checkout — Nova P300 ($100), 300C ($50), Amaran F22 ($30), Spotlight ($25), smoke machine ($15). There are no cameras on site, so bring your own gear.

Does my time include setup and teardown?

Yes — your block covers load-in through load-out, and the space should be reset how you found it. Book the buffer you need.

Can I book something recurring or longer than a day?

Yes — reach out on Instagram @jenelstudios or send an inquiry through Taylormade Creative and we'll set it up personally.
