Before you book anywhere

What to compare besides the hourly rate.

  1. Weekend pricing. Rates can change by day of the week, so check the weekend price before you plan a Saturday shoot. At Jenel it's $60 an hour on weekdays and $75 on weekends.
  2. Setup and teardown. Ask whether load-in and load-out count toward your time. At Jenel they do, so book the buffer you need and reset the room the way you found it.
  3. Deposits and extra fees. Ask what's due up front and what gets added at the end. Jenel is paid by card when you book, with no deposit.
  4. Lights and gear. Some studios include lighting, others rent it. Jenel rents lights at checkout: Nova P300 $100, 300C $50, Amaran F22 $30, Spotlight $25, smoke machine $15. There are no cameras on site.
  5. The room and the light. Check the size and how much daylight it gets. Jenel's main room is 17 by 15 feet, opens onto a dining and kitchen area, and has big windows, hardwood floors, and track lighting.
  6. How booking works. Some spaces make you request a time and wait for approval. Jenel has a live calendar: pick an open slot, pay, and you're confirmed on the spot.
